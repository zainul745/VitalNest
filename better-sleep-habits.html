7 Simple Habits for Better Sleep

Good sleep is not luck. It is mostly a set of habits you repeat every day. Here are seven that are easy to start tonight.

In this guide
Keep a fixed schedule
Limit screens
Watch caffeine

1. Keep a fixed schedule

Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Your body clock learns the pattern and sleepiness arrives on time.

2. Limit screens before bed

Stop using your phone at least 30 to 60 minutes before sleep. Bright light tells your brain it is still daytime.

3. Watch caffeine

Avoid tea, coffee and energy drinks in the afternoon. Caffeine can stay in your body for many hours.

4. Keep the room cool and dark

A cool, quiet, dark room helps you fall asleep and stay asleep.

5. Move your body daily

A daily walk or light exercise improves sleep quality. Finish hard workouts a few hours before bed.

6. Eat lighter at night

Heavy, spicy meals close to bedtime can cause discomfort. Try to finish dinner 2 to 3 hours before sleep.

7. Build a wind-down routine

Reading, stretching or a warm shower tells your body that the day is over.

This article is for information only. If you have long-term sleep problems, please see a doctor.
